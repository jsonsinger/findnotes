Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. No account, no sign-up, nothing to pay.
Where are my notes kept?
On your phone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded and nothing syncs.
How do I get it?
Open app.findnotes.io on your phone and add it to your home screen.
Can I back it up?
Yes. Export everything to a file from Settings and restore it any time.
