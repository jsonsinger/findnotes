Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. No account, no sign-up, nothing to pay. It's offered as is, so use it at your own risk.
Where are my notes kept?
On your phone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded and nothing syncs, so it stays private, and it's yours to look after.
How do I get it?
Tap Get FindNotes on your phone. The app shows you how to add it to your home screen.
Can I back it up?
Yes, and it's worth doing. Export everything from Settings now and then.
Is it finished?
Nearly. Everything works, but it is still being tested day to day, so things may change. See what's new.
